How this list is built

1. Evidence, not folklore

Vendor pricing pages and offer terms come first. Dated trackers are used for discovery and are labelled as tracker sources on the card. A blocked page means “stale”, not “expired”.

2. Lifecycle is explicit

Announced, scheduled, live, expired and archived are separate states. “Last verified” and “next check” are stored with every offer, so a year-round list does not quietly rot.

3. The renewal price is the point

A first-month price that renews high is a coupon, not a deal. Where the vendor prints a renewal price, it is on the card. Usefulness, not headline percentage, controls the default order.

4. Sponsorship is separated

Partner offers are labelled and never presented as independent editorial picks. No paid placement inside the Top picks ranking.
