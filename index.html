      How this list is built

      1. Vendor page or nothing

      A deal is listed only if the vendor's own pricing, promo or offer-terms page states it. Coupon aggregators, newsletters and "50% off" banners that lead to expired pages are excluded — that's why Grammarly is in the no-deal table with a live-looking banner.

      2. Dated, not timeless

      Every row carries the date it was checked, because last year's discount is not this year's. The window for this snapshot: 9–23 Nov 2026.

      3. The renewal price is the point

      A first-month price of $0.29 that renews at $15 is a coupon for one month, not a deal. Where the vendor prints a renewal price, it's on the card.

      4. First-party links only

      No affiliate tags, no redirects. Each card links the vendor page and the source where the offer was verified.
