## Deals you can buy now

Four of these sales end by December 4. The other six vendors give no end date, so they can stop without notice.

### Adobe Creative Cloud Pro: $29.99 a month for a year

Ends: Nov 27 · Who: individuals, teams, students

Adobe's Creative Cloud Pro costs $29.99 a month for the first year, down from $69.99, on an annual plan billed monthly. Students and teachers pay $15.99, and teams $44.99 per license. Pro includes Photoshop and Firefly along with the rest of the suite. The price goes back to $69.99 a month in year two, so set a reminder before the renewal date.

### ProWritingAid: half off yearly and lifetime Premium

Ends: Nov 29, 11:59 pm PT · Who: everyone; monthly plans aren't discounted

The editing tool sells Premium for $60 a year (was $120) and a lifetime license for $199.50 (was $399). Its pricing page counts down to November 29, though ProWritingAid's blog post gives December 4; go by the earlier date.

### ElevenLabs: Creator for $99, down from $220

Ends: Dec 1, 11:59 pm ET · Who: anyone not already on an annual Creator, Pro or higher plan

ElevenLabs cut its annual voice plans for the first year: Creator is $99 instead of $220 and Pro $445.50 instead of $990. Pro monthly subscribers who switch to annual pay $495. Catch: the second year renews at the full price.

### SE Ranking: 20% off annual SEO plans

Ends: Dec 4 · Who: new customers; existing ones get 20% off add-ons

SE Ranking's 20% comes on top of its usual annual-billing discount, with no code needed. It fits a small site owner who wants rank tracking and a site audit in one subscription.

### Higgsfield: up to 64% off annual plans

Ends: not stated · Who: annual plans

Higgsfield, the AI video and image studio, shows Plus at $33 a month billed yearly (was $59) and Ultra 3K at $50 (was $129). Its countdown pointed to November 20, but the same prices were still up when we checked that day, so the end date has moved at least once.

### Topaz Labs: bundles up to 67% off

Ends: not stated · Who: new and existing customers

Topaz's Studio Collection is $299 a year (was $499), and Topaz Photo alone is $169 (was $199). Existing subscribers can upgrade to every app for $99 a year. The sale saves $200 on the bundle and $30 on a single app, so buy the bundle only if you edit video too; for upscaling photos, Topaz Photo at $169 does the job.

### Luminar by Skylum: up to 77% off

Ends: not stated · Who: new buyers

Skylum's Luminar pricing page advertises "up to 77% OFF" this Black Friday on its Desktop, All Platforms and Max licenses. The page prices them in the visitor's local currency, so the dollar figure depends on where you are. Pick it over Adobe if you want a photo editor you pay for once and never subscribe to.
